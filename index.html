01 / ABOUT ADG // the part every other site fills with stock photos

A committee is a habit,
not a calendar.

We don't teach courses and we don't run the syllabus — that's the department's job, and they're better at it. ADG runs workshops, bootcamps, seminars, guest talks, competitions and one flagship hackathon where you build with what you're already being taught.

Deep Learning, NLP, GenAI & LLMs, Cloud, MLOps, Edge AI, AI-IoT — all already on the AIML scheme. You arrive with syllabus knowledge and leave with a working project, a certificate, a competition entry and receipts. Every session is open to every student; you do not need to be a member to walk in.
